= Access and Readiness
:navtitle: Access and Readiness

Verify that your environment is ready for the workshop.

== Log In to OpenShift

. Open the *OpenShift Console* tab on the right.
. Select *htpasswd* as the identity provider.
. Enter your credentials:
+
* *Username*: `{user}`
* *Password*: provided by your facilitator
. Confirm the OpenShift dashboard loads.

== Verify Terminal Access

. Open the *Terminal* tab on the right.
. Confirm you can run commands:
+
[source,role="execute"]
----
oc whoami
----
+
.Expected output
[source,text]
----
{user}
----

== Verify Maximo Access

. Open the *Maximo* tab on the right.
. Log in with your MAS credentials.
. Confirm the Maximo Navigator loads.

== Verify Your Project

Confirm your dedicated namespace exists:

[source,role="execute"]
----
oc get project student-{seat_number}
----

.Expected output
[source,text]
----
NAME                DISPLAY NAME   STATUS
student-{seat_number}                      Active
----

== Validation

Run the readiness check:

[source,role="execute"]
----
oc get nodes -o wide
----

Confirm all nodes show `Ready` status.

=== Verify

[%collapsible]
====
Your environment is ready when:

* You can log in to OpenShift Console
* `oc whoami` returns your username
* Maximo Navigator loads
* Your student namespace exists
* All nodes are `Ready`
====
