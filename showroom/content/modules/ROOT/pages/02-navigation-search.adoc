:navtitle: Navigation and Search

Explore the Maximo Application Suite interface and understand how it runs on OpenShift.

== Introduction

IBM Maximo Application Suite ({mas_version}) provides enterprise asset management capabilities. In this section, you will:

* Navigate the Maximo Suite Navigator
* Explore Maximo Manage
* Understand the relationship between MAS and OpenShift

== Exercise: Explore the MAS Navigator

. Open the *Maximo* tab on the right.
. In the Suite Navigator, locate the following applications:
+
* *Manage* — Enterprise Asset Management
* *Administration* — Suite configuration
. Click on *Manage* to open it.

== Exercise: Explore Maximo on OpenShift

MAS runs as a set of operators and custom resources on OpenShift.

. Switch to the *Terminal* tab.
. List the MAS namespaces:
+
[source,role="execute"]
----
oc get namespaces | grep mas
----
+
. View the MAS Suite custom resource:
+
[source,role="execute"]
----
oc get suite -n mas-inst1-core -o yaml | head -30
----
+
. Check the MAS operators:
+
[source,role="execute"]
----
oc get csv -n mas-inst1-core --no-headers
----

== Exercise: Explore OpenShift Resources

. In the *OpenShift Console* tab, navigate to *Workloads > Pods*.
. Select the `mas-inst1-core` project from the dropdown.
. Observe the MAS core pods running.
. Navigate to *Operators > Installed Operators*.
. Note the IBM operators managing MAS.
