= Navigation and Search
:navtitle: Navigation and Search
:duration: 10 minutes

Explore the OpenShift Console and learn how to navigate your cluster efficiently.

== Introduction

The OpenShift Console is your primary interface for managing and observing your cluster. In this section, you will:

* Add frequently used projects to your Favorites for quick access
* Use the built-in Search tool to find resources across your cluster

== Exercise: Add a Project to Favorites

OpenShift lets you mark frequently used projects as Favorites for quick access.

. Click the *Project* dropdown at the top of the console.
. In the dropdown list, find `mas-inst1-core`.
. Click the *star icon* next to `mas-inst1-core` to add it to your Favorites.
. Repeat for your student project `student-{seat_number}`.
. Click the *Project* dropdown again. Your favorited projects now appear at the top of the list under a *Favorites* section, making them easy to switch to.

== Exercise: Using the Search Tool

The Search tool lets you find any Kubernetes resource across your cluster.

. Click the *Search* icon (magnifying glass) in the left navigation menu of the OpenShift Console.
. In the *Resources* dropdown, select *Pod*.
. In the *Namespace* dropdown, select `mas-inst1-core`.
. Observe the list of pods returned. You can use the filter bar to narrow results further.
. Now change the *Resources* dropdown to *Deployment* and observe the deployments in the namespace.
. Try searching for other resource types:
+
* *Route* -- to see external endpoints
* *Service* -- to see internal networking
* *Secret* -- to see configuration secrets (contents are hidden by default)
+
. Change the namespace to `mas-inst1-manage` and search for *Pod* to see the Maximo Manage pods.
