= Access and Readiness
:navtitle: Access and Readiness
:duration: 5 minutes

Verify that your environment is ready for the workshop.

== Log In to OpenShift

. Open {openshift_console_url}[the OpenShift Console^] in your browser.
. On the login screen, select *Keycloak* as the identity provider.
. Enter your lab credentials:
+
* *Username*: `{lab_username}`
* *Password*: `{lab_password}`
+
These are shown on the Welcome page of this lab guide.
. Confirm the OpenShift web console dashboard loads and you can see the cluster overview.

== Verify Your Namespace

. In the OpenShift Console, click *Home* in the left navigation menu, then select *Projects*.
. In the filter bar at the top of the Projects list, type `student-{seat_number}`.
. Confirm that the project *student-{seat_number}* appears in the list and its status shows *Active*.
. Click on the *student-{seat_number}* project name to open it.
. Confirm the project overview page loads, showing resource details for your namespace.

== Verify Your Permissions

Your lab account can install operators and configure logging, but cannot modify MAS components. Confirm this by checking what you can see and do in the console.

. Make sure you are viewing the *student-{seat_number}* project (use the project selector at the top of the console if needed).
. Click *Workloads* in the left navigation menu, then select *Pods*.
. Confirm that the Pods page loads without an access error. You should see a list of pods (or "No Pods found" if none are running yet). This confirms you have view and create permissions in your student namespace.
. Now switch the project selector at the top of the console to *mas-inst1-core*.
. Click *Workloads* > *Pods* and confirm you can *view* the pods running in this namespace.
. Note that you have *read-only* access here -- you should not see options to delete or modify MAS resources. If you attempt to delete a pod, the console will display a permission denied error.

== Verify Cluster Health

. In the OpenShift Console, click *Home* in the left navigation menu, then select *Overview*.
. Review the *Cluster* section at the top. Confirm that the overall cluster status shows healthy (green indicators, no critical alerts).
. Check the *Status* card. All items (Cluster, Control Plane, Operators) should show a green checkmark or "OK" status.
. Now click *Compute* in the left navigation menu, then select *Nodes*.
. Review the list of nodes. Confirm that every node shows a *Ready* status in the Status column.
. If any node shows a status other than *Ready*, notify your facilitator.

=== Verify

[%collapsible]
====
Your environment is ready when:

* You can log in to the OpenShift Console using *Keycloak* with `{lab_username}`
* Your student project *student-{seat_number}* exists and shows *Active* status under *Home > Projects*
* You can view the *Pods* page under *Workloads* in your student namespace without access errors
* You *cannot* delete resources in the *mas-inst1-core* namespace
* The cluster overview under *Home > Overview* shows healthy status
* All nodes under *Compute > Nodes* show *Ready*
====
