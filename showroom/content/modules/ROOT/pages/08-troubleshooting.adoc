= Troubleshooting
:navtitle: Troubleshooting

Common issues and resolution steps for the workshop environment, using the OpenShift Console.

== Viewing Pod Logs

The OpenShift Console provides built-in log viewing for individual pods.

. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the OpenShift Console.
. Select the namespace containing the pod you want to investigate (e.g., `mas-inst1-core`, `mas-inst1-manage`, or `openshift-logging`).
. Click on the pod name to open its details page.
. Select the *Logs* tab to view the pod's log output.
. Use the following controls on the Logs tab:
+
* *Container* dropdown -- if the pod has multiple containers, select the one you want to view
* *Download* -- save the current log output to a file
* *Expand* -- view the logs in a full-screen view for easier reading
* *Wrap lines* -- toggle line wrapping for long log entries
+
. For pods that are restarting, check the *Previous* log option (if available) to see logs from the previous container instance.

== Using LokiStack for Log Analysis

If you have completed the Operator Installation exercise and have a working LokiStack, you can use *Observe > Logs* for powerful log analysis that spans across pods and time.

=== Viewing Logs for Active Pods

. Navigate to *Observe* > *Logs* in the OpenShift Console.
. Enter a LogQL query to view logs from a specific namespace:
+
[source,text]
----
{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-manage"}
----
+
. Click *Run Query*. You will see logs from all pods in the Manage namespace, aggregated across the cluster.

=== Viewing Logs for Restarted or Deleted Pods

One of the most powerful features of centralized logging is the ability to view logs from pods that no longer exist. This is critical for post-mortem analysis.

. In *Observe* > *Logs*, enter a query filtering by pod name:
+
[source,text]
----
{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-manage"} | json | kubernetes_pod_name=~".*all.*"
----
+
. Expand the time range using the time picker to include the period when the old pod was running.
. You will see logs from both current and previous pod instances -- the historical logs are preserved in Loki even after the pod was deleted and replaced.

=== Searching for Errors and Warnings

. Use LogQL filters to search for specific patterns:
+
* Error logs: `\{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-manage"} |= "error"`
* Warning logs: `\{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-manage"} |= "WARN"`
* Specific component: `\{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-core"} |= "reconcile"`
+
. Combine filters for more targeted searches:
+
[source,text]
----
{kubernetes_namespace_name="mas-inst1-manage"} |= "error" != "healthcheck"
----
+
This shows error logs while excluding routine healthcheck entries.

== OpenShift Console Not Loading

. Navigate to *Networking* > *Routes* in the OpenShift Console.
. Use the *Project* dropdown to select `openshift-console`.
. Verify the console route exists and has a valid host URL.
. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `openshift-console` namespace.
. Check that the console pods are in *Running* status.
. If pods are not Running, click on a pod to view its *Events* tab for error details.

== Cannot Log In

. Verify you are selecting *Keycloak* (not htpasswd) as the identity provider on the login screen.
. Confirm you are using the credentials shown on the Welcome page of this lab guide.
. If authentication fails, navigate to *Administration* > *Cluster Settings* > *Configuration* tab > *OAuth* to verify the Keycloak identity provider is listed.
. Check the pods in the `openshift-authentication` namespace via *Workloads* > *Pods* -- all authentication pods should be *Running*.
. If your login still fails, contact your facilitator to verify your Keycloak account.

== Maximo Not Accessible

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* in the `mas-inst1-core` project.
. Verify the IBM MAS operators show *Succeeded* status.
. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `mas-inst1-manage` namespace.
. Check that all Manage pods are in *Running* status. Pods showing *CrashLoopBackOff* or *Error* indicate a problem.
. Click on any unhealthy pod to view its *Logs* and *Events* tabs for error details.

== Operator Installation Issues

If you encounter problems installing operators in the Operator Installation exercise:

=== Subscription Not Progressing

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* and select the relevant namespace (e.g., `openshift-logging`).
. Check if the operator status shows *UpgradePending* or *Installing*.
. Click on the operator name, then select the *InstallPlan* tab.
. If an install plan is not approved, click on it and approve it manually.
. If the operator remains stuck, try uninstalling it and reinstalling from OperatorHub.

=== Operator Not Reaching "Succeeded"

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* in the relevant namespace.
. Click on the operator that is not reaching *Succeeded* status.
. Check the *Conditions* section on the operator details page for error messages.
. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the operator's namespace and look for pods that are not in *Running* status.
. Click on any unhealthy pod and check its *Logs* and *Events* tabs.

=== Permission Denied

If you get a "forbidden" error when installing an operator, contact your facilitator -- your lab account may not have the correct RBAC permissions.

== LokiStack Issues

=== LokiStack Not Becoming Ready

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* in the `openshift-logging` namespace.
. Click on *Loki Operator*, then click the *LokiStack* tab.
. Click on `logging-loki` to view its status and conditions. Look for error messages in the *Conditions* section.
. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `openshift-logging` namespace.
. Filter for Loki pods (look for names starting with `logging-loki-`).
. Click on any pod that is not *Running* and check its *Logs* and *Events* tabs.

=== S3 Credential Issues

If LokiStack fails with access errors:

. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Secrets* in the `openshift-logging` namespace.
. Find and click on `logging-loki-s3`.
. Click *Reveal values* to check that the bucket name, region, and credentials match what is shown on the Welcome page.
. If the values are incorrect, delete the secret and recreate it following the steps in the Operator Installation exercise.

== Logging Queries Return No Results

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* in the `openshift-logging` namespace.
. Click on *Red Hat OpenShift Logging*, then check the *ClusterLogForwarder* tab. Verify the `collector` forwarder exists and its conditions show *Ready*.
. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `openshift-logging` namespace. Verify that collector pods are running on each node.
. Check the LokiStack status (see LokiStack Issues above).
. If logs are missing, wait 2-3 minutes for log ingestion and retry your query.

== Keycloak Not Accessible

. Navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `masworld-keycloak` namespace.
. Verify that Keycloak pods are in *Running* status.
. Navigate to *Networking* > *Routes* in the `masworld-keycloak` namespace.
. Verify the route exists and click the *Location* URL to test access.

== Group Sync Issues

If the Group Sync Operator is not creating OpenShift groups:

. Navigate to *Operators* > *Installed Operators* in the `group-sync-operator` namespace.
. Verify the Group Sync Operator shows *Succeeded* status.
. Click on the operator, then check the *GroupSync* tab. Click on your GroupSync CR to view its status and conditions.
. Check the operator pod logs: navigate to *Workloads* > *Pods* in the `group-sync-operator` namespace, click on the operator pod, and review the *Logs* tab for LDAP connection errors.
. Verify the LDAP bind password secret exists in the `group-sync-operator` namespace under *Workloads* > *Secrets*.
